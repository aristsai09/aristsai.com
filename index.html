about

Hey! I’m Aris Tsai, a student researcher whose work spans computational chemistry, computational biology, genomics, structural biology, and biomedical engineering.

I’m interested in using computation to understand molecular mechanisms and improve biomedical prediction. My work has included machine-learning approaches to chemical toxicity, protein-structure and molecular-dynamics studies, computational genomics, and genome-engineering research.

I have presented computational research in toxicology and structural biology at the WACBE World Congress on Bioengineering and have worked on research projects involving UC Irvine and Stanford-affiliated programs.

Outside the lab, I work on science and education accessibility, play violin, and compete in golf.
